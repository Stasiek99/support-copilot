# Returns FAQ

## Return window

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused, unworn and still have their
tags and original packaging. After 30 days returns are not accepted, except for items that arrive
damaged or defective.

## How to start a return

Support emails the customer a prepaid return label. The customer packs the item, attaches the label
and drops the parcel at any carrier point. Returns are free of charge for the customer when the item
is damaged, defective or sent by mistake. Otherwise the standard return fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refunds after a return

The refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item
reaches our warehouse and passes inspection. The customer receives a confirmation email when the refund
is processed. It can take the bank a few additional days to show the money.

## Damaged or defective items

For a damaged or defective item, ask the customer for a photo of the damage. Once the photo is received
we send a prepaid return label and refund the full price, including the original delivery fee.
