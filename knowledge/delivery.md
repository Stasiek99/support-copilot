# Delivery FAQ

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 2-4 business days within the country and 5-8 business days within the EU.
Express delivery arrives the next business day for orders placed before 14:00. Customers receive a
tracking number by email when the parcel ships.

## Parcel delayed or not arriving

If tracking has not changed for more than 3 business days or the parcel is late, ask the customer for
the tracking number. Open an investigation with the carrier and update the customer within 24 hours.
If the parcel has not arrived after the investigation, we send a replacement or issue a full refund,
whichever the customer prefers.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed only before the order has shipped. Ask the customer for the new
address, update the order, and confirm the change by email. After the parcel has shipped, the address
can no longer be changed, but the customer can redirect the parcel through the carrier's tracking page.

## Damaged or missing items on arrival

If a parcel arrives damaged or an item is missing, ask for photos of the packaging and the item.
We send a replacement or refund once the photos are received. Customers do not need to return items
that are missing or unusable.
