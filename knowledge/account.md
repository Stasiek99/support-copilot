# Account FAQ

## Password reset

Customers can reset their password from the login page. The reset email arrives within a few minutes
and the link is valid for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder, confirm
the address on the account, and resend the email. If it still does not arrive, verify the customer's
identity and reset access manually.

## Verifying identity

Before changing account details or resetting access manually, verify the customer by asking for the
email address on the account and the last order number. Never ask for passwords or full card numbers.

## Account and data deletion

Customers can request deletion of their account and personal data at any time. Verify the customer's
identity first, then send a confirmation link to the registered email address. After the customer
confirms, the account and personal data are deleted within 30 days. Legally required records, such as
invoices, are retained for the period required by law. An agent must not mark the request as completed
before the deletion has actually taken place.

## Changing email address

The email address on an account can be changed after identity verification. A confirmation link is
sent to both the old and the new address, and the change takes effect once the new address is confirmed.
