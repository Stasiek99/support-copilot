# Payments FAQ

## Declined card payments

If a card payment is declined at checkout, the order is not placed. The amount shown on the bank
statement is usually a temporary authorization hold, not a charge. The hold is released
automatically within 3-5 business days. Customers do not need to pay again for the same order
unless they want to place it with a different payment method.

## Duplicate charges

If a customer sees two identical charges for a single order, the second transaction is refunded.
Agents flag the duplicate transaction for a refund, and it appears on the customer's statement
within 5-7 business days. The customer receives a confirmation email once the refund has been
processed.

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank transfer. Bank transfers
are confirmed within 1-2 business days, and the order ships after the payment is confirmed.

## Invoices and receipts

An invoice is emailed automatically after every successful payment. Customers can request a copy
of any invoice from support, and company details can be added to an invoice before the order ships.
